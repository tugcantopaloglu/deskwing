Privacy in your workspace.

This describes DeskWing's data paths and the features users can choose. Features vary by release; review the notes for the particular build you install.

Last reviewed · 6 November 2026

DeskWing is developed by Tuğcan Topaloğlu. For privacy or support questions, email tugcan@tugcan.dev. General technical issues can also be discussed through the DeskWing GitHub project.

Paired devices and controls

DeskWing connects your tablet to the Windows PC you choose. The core workflow does not require a DeskWing cloud account. Pairing uses a code/PIN and a verified certificate fingerprint; subsequent requests use the saved device credential over the encrypted connection.

Android can optionally use the camera when you choose Scan pairing QR. Camera frames are decoded locally in memory for the pairing code; DeskWing does not save or send those camera images to the PC or a remote service. The camera closes when the scanner leaves the foreground. You can deny camera permission and enter the pairing details manually.

Pairing details are stored on your devices. The companion retains its saved tablet list and local certificate. Board labels, configured action identifiers, app/media state, and requests needed to operate the selected tools travel between the tablet and companion. The project maintainer does not receive that content through the core pairing connection.

PC configuration can contain private app paths, sound references, labels, shortcuts, and OBS connection details. Keep exports and backup files private unless you have reviewed what they contain.

Drawings, notes, and documents

Ink and text are stored locally, with recovery copies where supported. While the shared writing workspace is connected, its document content is transferred to the paired companion and retained in the companion's private data directory. Offline edits remain on the tablet until sharing resumes. Conflicting versions need a user choice.

In builds with notebooks, named documents, pages, rendered backgrounds, annotations, notes, thumbnails, and history can contain personal or sensitive information. They synchronize only with the paired PC over the authenticated encrypted connection.

PDF import starts with a file you select and renders its pages locally. The import pipeline does not upload the original PDF to the PC or an OCR/cloud service. Rendered page images and the ink/notes you add can then be shared with your paired PC as notebook content. Selecting a file from a cloud-backed document provider is separately governed by that provider.

Importing a file is not permission to publish it. Exporting or sharing a drawing/document creates a copy at the destination you select; that destination may have its own synchronization or retention rules.

DeskWing's handwriting entry uses your installed Android keyboard/IME or the native iPadOS text-entry/Scribble facilities. Availability, language support, offline operation, and any provider processing depend on those tools. DeskWing does not operate a remote handwriting-recognition service.

Display capture and input

Starting a preview or display session sends pixels from its explicitly authorized Windows target to the tablet. A window target can include the selected application's client content; a display target includes visible content on that selected display. Second display uses the app-owned extra monitor with explicit PC sharing. Avoid placing sensitive material on a shared target.

Video and preview frames are transient: the core viewer does not create a saved screen recording or upload frames to the maintainer. Closing the session, backgrounding, losing its authorization, or changing its target ends that session. A recent frame can remain briefly during bounded recovery; stopped or invalid sessions clear their image.

When an input-enabled build/session is explicitly authorized, pen pressure/tilt, pointer/touch coordinates, button/contact state, and lifecycle releases are sent to the companion to control the selected Windows target. The receiving Windows application handles the resulting actions and saves its own document. Pen marks made in another Windows app do not automatically become a DeskWing notebook.

Optional services and providers

Twitch and Kick viewers
Opening an optional provider view contacts that provider and may use its cookies/storage, account state, and network services. Provider content has no DeskWing PC-control bridge. Review the provider's own privacy information before signing in or sharing content.
Updates and downloads
The Windows update workflow contacts GitHub for release metadata and downloads. Mobile beta/store distribution can contact Apple or Google. Those services receive the connection/request information associated with providing their service.
Keyboard and export destinations
Your keyboard/IME, operating system, selected file/share destination, and any cloud storage you choose apply their own privacy and retention settings.

These optional connections are separate from the paired-PC workflow. Their policies are available from Twitch, Kick, GitHub, Apple, and Google.

Diagnostics and support

The app can display connection, timing, frame-count, error, and recovery information. These diagnostics help distinguish an unavailable source, disconnected PC, output-routing choice, and a failed action. They are not an automatic support upload.

If you choose to contact the developer, review everything you send. GitHub issues are public. Do not include pairing PINs, tokens, certificate private keys, OBS passwords, private documents, full board exports, personal file paths, or unreviewed logs. A minimal description, app/OS versions, and redacted error text are usually sufficient.

Content you deliberately post to GitHub is handled by GitHub and visible according to the place you post it. For a private privacy question or data-removal request, contact tugcan@tugcan.dev. Email is processed by the sender's and recipient's mail providers; send only the information needed for the request.

Retention, unpairing, and deletion

  • Stop access: exit the active pen/display/control session. Turn off PC sharing when it is no longer needed.
  • Remove a pairing: use Forget this PC on the tablet and revoke the tablet from the companion's tablet-connections list. These are separate device-side records.
  • Remove local content: use the content/history controls offered by your build. A removed document or page can still exist in recovery/history, board backups, or an exported file.
  • Remove all companion data: stop the companion, preserve anything you want to keep, then remove its private Windows data directory and any copies/backups you retained. Uninstalling program files alone does not guarantee deletion of saved boards, pairings, or documents.
  • Remove tablet data: use the operating system's app-data removal controls where available. On iOS, use Forget this PC before removal to explicitly delete the saved Keychain pairing. Also remove exports or copies from destinations you selected.

Local data is generally retained until you remove it or the configured recovery/backup history replaces it. Your exported files, Windows application documents, provider accounts, and third-party backups are controlled separately from DeskWing.
