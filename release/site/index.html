An extension of your PC.

Keep useful controls, a place to write, and another view of your desktop beside you.

DeskWing pairs a tablet with a Windows companion for streaming controls, app shortcuts, Soundpad, shared ink and notes, Windows pen input, and optional second-display viewing.

Set up your workspaceCheck available builds

Windows companion required. Android hardware testing is underway; iOS beta distribution is being prepared.

  • Control your desk

    Build boards for OBS, audio, sounds, apps, shortcuts, and repeatable workflows.

  • Write beside your work

    Use shared ink, text notes, and compatible platform handwriting tools. Draw into an explicitly selected Windows target.

  • Keep another view nearby

    View a separately created Windows desktop with native video and clear sharing controls.

Beta release preparation

These pages are prepared for review. They do not announce an App Store, Google Play, or TestFlight release. Published GitHub builds may precede the latest development features; read each release's notes before installing.

Your PC is the workspace.

The core connection runs between your paired devices over encrypted local Wi-Fi/LAN or supported USB connections. A DeskWing cloud account is not required. Optional provider pages, update services, and your handwriting keyboard have their own data handling.

Read how data is handled

Know what your setup needs.

Pen behavior depends on your device and the Windows app. Second display needs a compatible virtual-display driver already installed on the PC. Refresh selection is a target; the viewer reports actual delivery separately.
